About Canopy360

Canopy360 is a Smart Tree Care & Sustainability Platform built as a college project. It helps people keep track of the trees they plant and look after, so that every tree gets the right care at the right time.

The platform uses Python and Flask with a MySQL database. Carbon values are simplified estimates for learning, not exact scientific measurements.

  • Easy to use for anyone
  • Works with real weather data
  • Reports you can download and share
  • Built to support a greener future
