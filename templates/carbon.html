Easy ways to measure (optional)

  • Trunk: wrap a string or tape once around the trunk at chest height, then measure the string in cm and choose "I measured around the trunk".
  • Height: compare with a building. One storey is about 3 to 4 m.
  • Not sure? Leave both on "estimate". The result is less accurate but still useful.

Calculation Method

  1. Biomass = 0.0509 × 0.6 (wood density) × diameter² × height
  2. Carbon stored = biomass × 0.5
  3. CO₂ stored = carbon × 3.67
  4. CO₂ per year = CO₂ stored ÷ tree age

This is a simplified estimate for educational purposes, not an exact scientific measurement.
